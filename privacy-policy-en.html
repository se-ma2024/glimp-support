Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Glimp ("the App") handles your data. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information.

  • All notes and ideas (Seeds) you create are stored only on your device.
  • No data is transmitted to external servers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • No advertisements are displayed.

Data Storage

All data created in the App is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. The developer has no access to this data.

Third-Party Sharing

Since the App does not collect any data, no data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any data, no personal information from children is collected.
